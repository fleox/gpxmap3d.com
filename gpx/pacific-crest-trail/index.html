The Pacific Crest Trail is one of America's great long-distance trails, crossing the western United States through California, Oregon and Washington between the Mexican border and Canada. This track covers it in full, north to south, over about 4,175 km.

The figures speak for themselves: nearly 230,000 m of ascent, altitude ranging from 25 m to 4,001 m, and around 167 days of walking. From the Cascades to the Sierra Nevada and the southern deserts, the trail strings together very different landscapes over almost six months.
