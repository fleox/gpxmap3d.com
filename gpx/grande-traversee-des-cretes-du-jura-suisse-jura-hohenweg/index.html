The Jura Hohenweg, or Jura Crest Trail, follows the limestone range of the Swiss Jura along its full length, from near Zurich to the Lake Geneva region. This track of about 320 km runs one way from east to west and is generally split into thirteen stages.

It never goes above 1,689 m, yet it piles up nearly 17,600 m of ascent and the same in descent, as it keeps linking ridges, hollows and passes. It is a long mid-mountain trek where endurance over many days matters more than altitude.
