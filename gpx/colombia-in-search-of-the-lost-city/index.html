Deep in the Sierra Nevada de Santa Marta in northern Colombia, this route heads for the Lost City, or Ciudad Perdida, an archaeological site built by the Tayrona people centuries before Europeans arrived. The track lies in the Magdalena department, within the municipality of Santa Marta, near Mutanyi.

The loop covers about 49 km with close to 3,500 m of total ascent, between roughly 150 m and 1,220 m above sea level. Planned over two days here, it is a sustained walk with a great deal of repeated climbing and descending before returning to its starting point.
